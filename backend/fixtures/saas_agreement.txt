SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE (SAAS) ENTERPRISE AGREEMENT

This SaaS Agreement is made effective as of June 1, 2025, between CloudMatrix AI Corp ("Vendor") and Horizon Enterprises ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION GRANT AND ACCEPTABLE USE
Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription license to access the CloudMatrix platform solely for Customer's internal corporate operations.

2. FEES AND BILLING
2.1. Customer shall pay an annual enterprise fee of $18,000 billed annually in advance. All invoices are payable within net 15 days of invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
3.1. The Initial Term shall be one (1) year.
3.2. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year renewal terms unless Customer delivers written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

4. EARLY TERMINATION CONVENIENCE
4.1. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time without penalty by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
5.1. In no event shall Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the total fees actually paid by Customer in the one (1) month immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability ($1,500.00).
5.2. Customer's liability for indemnification and breach of proprietary rights shall be completely uncapped.

6. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
6.1. Customer shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor from any third-party claims arising from Customer data, integration code, or system misuse.
