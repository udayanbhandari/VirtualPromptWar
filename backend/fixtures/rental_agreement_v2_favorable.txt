RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (TENANT-FAVORABLE AMENDMENT)

This Residential Lease Agreement Amendment is entered into as of January 15, 2025, by and between Oakridge Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant the residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield ("Premises").

2. TERM
The lease term commences on February 1, 2025, and concludes on January 31, 2026 ("Initial Term").

3. RENT AND PAYMENT
3.1. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $2,400.00, payable on the first day of each calendar month with a five (5) day grace period and no late charges before the sixth day.

4. EARLY TERMINATION BY TENANT
4.1. Tenant shall have the unrestricted right to terminate this Agreement prior to expiration of the Initial Term by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice to Landlord, with zero penalty, no lease break fee, and full deposit return.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
5.1. Tenant has deposited $2,400.00 with Landlord, held in an interest-bearing escrow account, fully refundable within fourteen (14) days of lease end.

6. ACCESS AND INSPECTION
6.1. Landlord may enter the Premises only upon at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, during reasonable business hours, and only with Tenant's prior consent.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
7.1. Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other solely for direct damages caused by their own willful misconduct or gross negligence.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1. Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the local Small Claims Court or through mutual mediation in Springfield, with each party bearing their own legal expenses.
