RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025, by and between Oakridge Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant the residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield ("Premises").

2. TERM
The lease term commences on February 1, 2025, and concludes on January 31, 2026 ("Initial Term").

3. RENT AND PAYMENT
3.1. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $2,400.00, payable on the first day of each calendar month.
3.2. If rent is not received by the second day of the month, Tenant shall pay an immediate late fee of $150.00 plus a continuing charge of $25.00 for each calendar day the balance remains unpaid.

4. EARLY TERMINATION BY TENANT
4.1. Tenant shall have the express right to terminate this Agreement prior to expiration of the Initial Term by providing at least thirty (30) days prior written notice to Landlord and paying a lease break fee equal to one month's rent.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
5.1. Tenant has deposited $2,400.00 with Landlord as security for the faithful performance of Tenant's obligations hereunder.

6. ACCESS AND INSPECTION
6.1. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any time without prior notice or consent to inspect the property, perform alterations, or exhibit the Premises to prospective purchasers.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
7.1. Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and its agents from and against all liabilities, claims, damages, expenses, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from any occurrence within or about the Premises, regardless of whether caused by the negligence or fault of Landlord.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Lease shall be resolved exclusively through mandatory, binding arbitration conducted by a private arbitrator selected solely by Landlord, with Tenant waiving all rights to a jury trial and class action proceedings.

9. FORFEITURE AND ABSOLUTE TERM COMMITMENT
9.1. Neither party may terminate this Agreement prior to the complete expiration of the 12-month Initial Term for any reason whatsoever.
9.2. Any attempt by Tenant to vacate or surrender the Premises prior to January 31, 2026, shall constitute an irremediable breach, resulting in automatic forfeiture of the entire Security Deposit and immediate acceleration of all unpaid rent through the end of the 12-month term.
